Pokémon Bantso Manual

Walk and find Pokémon

Tap a map tile to walk there. Red and white Poké Ball markers point to wild Pokémon. Faceted gems mark collectible stones. You can also be ambushed along the way, and the wild Pokémon gets the first turn.

Open My Team

Tap the backpack to view your Pokémon, their HP, and your stone inventory. Tap a healthy Pokémon to make it active. Tap the trainer guide icon on a card to see its type matchups.

Level up

Catch or befriend different species. Every two new species raises your level, giving your team more HP and attack power. Collect 18 different species to unlock the League.

Attack

Choose a move and check its type matchup. Water attacks are strong against Fire, for example. Some types resist a move or are immune to it.

Catch or offer food

Weaken a wild Pokémon to improve your catch chance. Keep it above zero HP so you can still catch it. If you want to befriend it with food, try early. The chance drops as you take hits.

Swap or run

Swapping to a teammate uses your turn. Running can fail and give the opponent a free attack. A failed escape can also cost you a teammate.

Tap a stone to collect it

Tap the stone marker and your trainer will walk to it. A pickup message confirms it went into your backpack. One stone appears at a time and changes about every 12 seconds. Every type appears once per rotation.

💧 Water Stone
Look for a blue droplet marker along the river that crosses the map.
⚡ Thunder Stone
Look for a yellow lightning marker on a tile beneath the thundercloud, including forest tiles.
🔥 Fire Stone
Look for an orange stone among the small fires in the lower left.
🍃 Leaf Stone
Look for a green leaf marker on a tile with trees.
❄️ Ice Stone
Look for a snowflake marker on the glacier near the bottom of the map.
🌙 Moon Stone
Look for a crescent moon marker on the gray rocky outcrop on the right side of the map.
✨ Shiny Stone
Look for a pale gem with a pulsing glint. It can appear on any terrain.

Evolve from My Team

Open your backpack in the forest and tap the evolution you want on a Pokémon's card. If the button is disabled, the note underneath names the stone you need. An evolution uses one of the listed stones. Options with no stone listed can evolve without one.

Eevee's stone evolutions

  • Water Stone → Vaporeon
  • Thunder Stone → Jolteon
  • Fire Stone → Flareon
  • Leaf Stone → Leafeon
  • Ice Stone → Glaceon

More stone evolutions

Pikachu and Magneton use a Thunder Stone. Growlithe uses a Fire Stone, Jigglypuff uses a Moon Stone, and Togetic uses a Shiny Stone.

In Hard Mode, you also choose a teammate to release before the evolution goes through.

Challenge the League

Reach level 10 with 18 different species to unlock the League. Tap Fight to battle a Champion. League opponents get stronger as you win, and you can't catch them. Use Back to Forest to keep collecting stones after unlocking the League.

Hard Mode

Your team holds up to seven Pokémon. New teammates need two fights to settle in. After a teammate fights twice, it must rest for one fight. Your original Pikachu stays ready between fights, even after evolving into Raichu. Evolving also requires releasing another teammate, and your starter stays with you.

Starting over

Restart resets your progress and keeps your starter. New Game starts a fresh game. Choosing Hard Mode starts a fresh game with Hard Mode rules.
